# FarmRPG Next Steps

**Use it here: https://prlygrly.github.io/FarmRPG-Next-Steps/**

A free planner for [FarmRPG](https://farmrpg.com) players. Paste (or upload) a few pages from the game and it tells you what to do
next: the quickest masteries, quests and Tower levels to finish, where to spend your Arnold Palmers, Apple Ciders and Large Nets,
what to plant, what your farm will soon overflow with, what to cook, and what to toss in the wishing well today.

**How to use it**
1. Open the page and go to **Setup**.
2. In FarmRPG, open **Mastery Progress** (open every tier first), press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it into the box.
   On a phone, where copying doesn't work, save the page instead (Chrome: ⋮ → Download) and use **Upload a saved page**.
3. Add **My Inventory** and **Help Needed** the same way. Optional: **Perks**, **Farm Supply**, your **farm page** and the **Orchard**.
4. Look at **Next steps**, **Quests**, **Best use** and the other tabs.

**Skip the copying (desktop):** install [`farmrpg-auto-read.user.js`](farmrpg-auto-read.user.js) in Tampermonkey or
Violentmonkey and just play. Each game page you open is handed to the planner when its tab is in view; pages it doesn't
read are skipped. The script only reads pages you open yourself: it loads nothing from FarmRPG and clicks nothing.
With it on, a **Here** card above every tab gives the planner's advice for the game page you have open (Wishing Well
tosses, an item's keep / toss / sell, a quest's next step, Kitchen, Steak Market, pets, farm, exploring spots, Workshop, Farmer's Market, Temple, perk pages), and the
**Checklist** ticks the "look at" items (Item of the Day, Mercantile, gallery…) when you open those pages.

Everything stays in your own browser: nothing you paste is uploaded or sent anywhere. Hover (or tap) the ⓘ marks for explanations.

**Data** comes from [buddy.farm](https://buddy.farm), the [FarmRPG wiki](https://farmrpg.com/wiki.php) and
[farmrpg-trade.live](https://farmrpg-trade.live) (trade prices, unofficial). This is a fan-made tool, not affiliated with FarmRPG.

## For developers

Plain static site: `index.html` + JS files, no build step. Effort is counted in AP (1 Apple Cider = 1 Large Net = 1 AP) plus time
waiting on daily production. Nothing player-specific is hardcoded; all player data comes from pastes or typed fields.
Before each push run `node tools/bump-version.js` (versions the script tags so
browsers never mix old and new files).

**Game rules and planner decisions live in [`knowledge/`](knowledge/index.md)** (Google's
[Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/knowledge-catalog/blob/main/okf/SPEC.md) v0.2): one page per
mechanic, one per data file, and `planner/rules.md`. Read the relevant page before changing game logic; add new mechanics there
with a source.

## Run / test
- Preview: `node test/serve.js`, then open http://localhost:5174 (any static server works).
- Tests: `for t in test/*.test.js; do node $t; done`.
- Fixtures (`test/fixtures/`) use made-up numbers and no chat or usernames. Keep it that way.
- In the page console: `__planner.state()`, `__planner.last()`, `__planner.render()`, `__planner.coster()`.

## Files
- `index.html`: the UI (tabs, rendering, saves, themes).
- `engine.js`: planning and costing (Tower, AK, gather vs craft, production, grinds, silver).
- `parse.js`: reads pasted pages; `detectPage()` says which page it is.
- `savecode.js`: compact save codes for QR codes.
- `farmrpg-auto-read.user.js`: the auto-read userscript. On FarmRPG it keeps each page's text (userscript storage); on the
  planner (`<meta name="frp-planner">`) it posts `frp-auto-page` messages, read once each through the quick paste.
- `tools-menu.js`: the ☰ menu; the net and bacon planners load it from this site.
- Data: `tower.js`, `drops.js`, `recipes.js`, `recipes-extra.js`, `seeds.js`, `meals.js`, `levels.js`, `quests.js`, `loot.js`,
  `npcs.js`, `items.js`, `trade.js` (snapshots, regenerated by `tools/snapshot-*.js`); `ww.js`, `seasons.js`, `prices.js`,
  `upgrades.js` (hand-kept). Each is described in `knowledge/data/`.

## Tabs
- **Next steps**: what to do next, quickest first; Tower levels (next 10 to all, AK-only levels can fold); AK plan; Leveling up.
- **Quests**: what each quest still needs, its cost and reward (titles fold an entry; "Show one" pins one quest's next step on top); the wishing well's free tosses for today.
- **Best use**: where to spend nets and drinks, trips (what a planned exploring trip brings), what to plant, daily overflow, before you collect pet items (what would void), kitchen, silver goal, exploring drinks (items per gold: Arnold Palmers, Apple Ciders or Orange Juice), perks to get next (each perk for sale on your Perks and Farm Supply pages, ranked by the AP it saves on your plan per point or gold).
- **Checklist**: daily and monthly reset checklists (from the Library), and the Steak Market (sell Truffles today?).
- **Reference**: how the game works as the planner understands it (pigs, the Temple, bank interest, exploring drinks…), with sources; searchable.
- **Slow grinds**: the Tower masteries that take months, what limits them, what an upgrade would save, and what maxed buildings would cost and save.
- **Veggie Juice**: a goal, the next step and the cycle as a checklist (Boost crops, Tomato and Watermelon, Horn / Twine /
  Glass Bottle, Beets); a calculator.
- **Setup**: pastes, perks, general settings, places, production.
- **⚙ App settings** (header): theme; Detail (Monotask: one thing per section / Condensed / Expanded: labelled points);
  Grid or one-column layout; the Truffle line above every tab; Best use order; undo a paste; save codes. Kept on this device, apart from game data.
- Anywhere: Ctrl+V outside a text box reads a page; ▾ folds a card (remembered); keys 1–7 switch tabs; phones get a bottom tab bar.

## Roadmap
- Shared Truffle prices: one player's Steak Market paste gives everyone today's prices (needs a free Cloudflare Worker + KV;
  the Truffle prompt then says the two prices are shared). Optional Reddit hint for notable days.
- Place Info page paste: its "Things found here" list (on hand / cap) also updates those items' inventory counts,
  like the All Pet Items paste.
- Animal buildings (Chicken Coop, Pasture, Pig Pen): read their pages, animal prices included, so Slow grinds' Maxed line
  can price them. Waits on a Pig Pen copy after the next slaughter (with prices) and Coop / Pasture copies.
- Friendship: read the Friendship Levels page (levels, gifts to the next level).
- Parked: Grape Juices in days of the daily limit; Beets in cap-sized chunks per Grape Juice round.
- Ideas: a plan-by-place view;
  mining costs; Compass via Magna Core in the well as a crafting loop.
